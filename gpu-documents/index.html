GPU Documents

Four declassified documents from the Branch State Archives of the Ukrainian Foreign Intelligence Service (SZRU), FISU F.1, Case 7408, Operation Nadiya. Source: SZRU, published 23 November 2025.

Intelligence Summary — 1932-11-14

FISU — F.1 — Case 7408 — Vol. 4 — P. 8

From: INO OOS GPU

To: Japanese intelligence (intercepted)

GPU intelligence summary No. 23 (14 Nov 1932), compiled for Japanese diplomatic services, cites Ukraïns'kyi Khliborob as source for information about executions of starving peasants during the Holodomor.

Intercepted Letter — 1930-10-27

FISU — F.1 — Case 7408 — Vol. 4

From: GPU agent, Istanbul

To: Editor, Ukraïns'kyi Khliborob, Brazil

Letter intercepted by GPU sent to the editor of Khliborob from an Istanbul GPU contact, requesting information about the Ukrainian community in Brazil and acknowledging receipt of the newspaper.

Intercepted Letter — 1930-11-23

FISU — F.1 — Case 7408

From: Ukraïns'kyi Narodnyi Soiuz, São Caetano, São Paulo

To: Ukrainian Hromada, Istanbul

Letter from the previously undocumented Ukrainian People's Union (São Caetano, São Paulo), intercepted by GPU, describing the organisation's anti-Soviet programme and seeking coordination with the Istanbul Ukrainian community.

Intercepted Letter — 1932-02-22

FISU — F.1 — Case 7408

From: Ukraïns'kyi Tsentr v Kuritiba, Brazil

To: Ukrainian Society, Istanbul

Letter from the Ukrainian Centre in Curitiba intercepted by GPU, referencing a Christmas greeting published in Khliborob No. 3 (31 Jan 1932) and requesting ongoing correspondence with Istanbul.

Preserved by SUBRAS (Sociedade Ucraniana do Brasil). Digital archive created as part of a research project at the University of Münster.
