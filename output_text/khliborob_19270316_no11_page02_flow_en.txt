THE PATH OF LABOR.
(A psychological turning point).
The outcome of the December General Meeting of the Ukrainian Union had a depressing effect on some of our colonies.
Everyone remembers the organization 'Zori' (Stars), the organization 'Narodna Rada' (People's Council) with 'Ukraina' (Ukraine), everyone remembers not only the flourishing, but also their end, and therefore many people thought that with the Ukrainian Union, the old story begins, that it is starting to decline.
This impression was exploited, as was to be expected, by 'Pratsia' (Labor) and its collaborators, who spread a handful of the most diverse rumors, hoping to overthrow the hated Administrative Body of the Ukrainian Union, because, as they thought, the time had come to break the 'nationalist fictions' and cleanse the Ukrainian Union, as they put it, 'of barricades' and all adherents of 'nationalist movement' ideas.
On the other hand, they hoped that it would be easy for them to change the direction of the Ukrainian Union towards communism.
Through its active units in the colonies, primarily through its readers and distributors of 'Pratsia', who at meetings, during encounters with people, and in various newspaper articles, and especially in letters, calculating that the editorial board of 'U. Kh.' (Ukrainian Farmer) does not respond to such literature, and therefore the silence from 'U. Kh.' is a sign of agreement with their ideas, which they disseminated among their readers and distributors, and then among the broad masses of the Ukrainian population.
Even in close circles of the union, among these readers, units were found who helped to spread such rumors and reinforced the impression that everything was falling apart, everything was going nowhere, that no honest person could engage with the Ukrainian Union, nor help it, because a collapse of all our achievements, etc., etc., must happen.
For some time, some colonies froze: they expected something extraordinary, and therefore waited to see what turn the whole matter would take.
So January passed, and the beginning of February. During this time, nothing extraordinary happened, nor could it have happened: 'U. Kh.' continued to be published as before, regardless of any harassment from 'Pratsia' and its other 'friends,' and although there were some flaws in it, our secret is already so understandable that they can be forgiven, because they are explained by the inexperience of the new editor, who never had anything to do with this before, who took this position only by fulfilling his civic duty.
In place of some discouraged colonies, others full of activity came, and this activity not only covered the discouraged, but also provided an excess of strength. While we had to count new subscribers.
And as is happening everywhere, so it is with us and can be. This is the so-called psychological turning point in the recognition of work, which occurred among the majority in the period between January and February, because the minority, during this time, finally became convinced that work is progressing, that it (the minority) will support whatever decision is made, and will act for the good of the Ukrainian Union. A similar break and from the second half of February, revival and movement began in all colonies. The revival of life is evident from the fact that 'Ukrainian Farmer' has 712 certain subscribers and 140 readers, with issue No. 10 of its periodical 'Ukr. Kh.' held back from subscribers.
And as it was mentioned, those 712 subscribers and 140 readers are not all. To them must be added those who have already paid for 1927, and their number is no less than 500, so 'Ukr. Kh.' has more than 1300 subscribers and 200 readers. And this in a short time (in 2 ½ months).
But not only that, there are also unreliable ones. In the last year, in which the Ukrainian Union acted as it pleased, it did a lot of damage without fault, because it did not carry out that active work that should have given it more than 1000 subscribers and 200 readers, which would have enabled it to publish the periodical 'Ukr. Kh.' three times a month. And then no one could say no, being with the seeds.
And there were complaints that the administration of 'Ukr. Kh.' withheld 'Ukr. Kh.' from them, because he was so used to it that he is their dear guest-advisor and will tell how people live in the world, and will teach how to live and what path to follow, it flowed cheerfully, joyfully and beautifully together with the readers and by the sum of 'victory-fate' and together with them by our achievements. The thing is, they will complain and lose, but let it be at first, or over whom, or not over themselves? It is conducted by a quiet, unhurried autonomous work, because we know that only one thing will save us from trouble, that work costs both strength and... money. 'Ukr. Khliborob' (Ukrainian Farmer) also needs them and cannot give itself away. Whoever remains from the subscribers, that will be for all of us and will be distributed, or cheaply to its readers, and if it runs out, the situation is worse: no one wants to.
POTATO JUBILEE.
This year marks 360 years since the potato was brought from America to Europe, where it became fully adopted and widespread. Until then, although it had been introduced to other countries, it was used very little. For 250 years, that is, until the second half of the 18th century, the potato was only on the tables of monarchs and for their pleasure, and it was considered a plant suitable for consumption only in times of famine.
But after that time, the potato quickly spread, and in the 18th century it really came to Ukraine.
Now, potato cultivation has great significance in agriculture, which each of us understands. The potato is an important food product for humans and for farm animals.

SHOULD THE FARMER KEEP ACCOUNTS AND FARM RECORDS?

A healthy community must consist of strong units, to which individual citizens, as well as various classes and strata, including farmers, belong, on whom all other strata among us rely.
Every worker, capable and diligent, not faint-hearted, will be able to earn enough to support himself and his family, raise and bring up his children. Someone may point out that today for a worker and his labor, and his demands have grown immensely high.
