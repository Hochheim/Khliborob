Хлібороб · O Lavrador

Digital archive of Khliborob / O Lavrador — the Ukrainian diaspora newspaper in Brazil (1924–present)

Pilot corpus: this demo currently contains 8 of an estimated 14,000+ pages held by SUBRAS (2 issues), digitized to demonstrate the pipeline as part of a grant application.

Khliborob (Хлібороб, 'The Farmer'), founded in Porto União, Paraná in 1924 and published as O Lavrador since the 1940s, is one of the longest-running Ukrainian diaspora newspapers in the world. For a century it has chronicled the life of the Ukrainian community in Brazil — its parishes and schools, its politics, and its ties to Ukraine through war, famine, and independence. This archive presents digitized issues with searchable Ukrainian transcripts, English and Portuguese translations, and indices of the people and places they mention. Every page scan is preserved on IPFS and anchored on the Polygon blockchain, alongside declassified Soviet intelligence files documenting the newspaper's surveillance during the Holodomor.
