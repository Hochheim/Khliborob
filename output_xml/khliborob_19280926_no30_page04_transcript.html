Two scholars, Welsche and Bandon, state that great changes will occur in all of Europe in the near future. The signs of these changes are the numerous earthquakes, volcanoes, huge snowfalls, and the world has been visited by these recently. Due to these earth tremors and common occurrences, fanatics of the Baltic Soviet fleet managed to retrieve the British submarine (boat) from the bottom of the Azov Gulf, where it had been sunk by Soviet warships. The submarine, pulled from the water, was towed to Kronstadt and placed in the bay. The submarine remained underwater for almost 9 years, and its hull was thickly covered with rust. This is one of the most powerful vessels of its type. Its construction was completed in 1919. Its displacement is 1150 tons. It has 6 torpedo tubes and 6 rapid-fire guns. Skeletons of British sailors were found inside the submarine's compartments. It is believed that about 40 souls perished on board. On the submarine's upper deck, there is evidence that it was struck by a cannon. Further investigation will reveal other damage and the causes of its sinking. The preliminary…
