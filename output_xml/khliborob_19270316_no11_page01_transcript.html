From Brazil.

Emigrants on coffee plantations of electors, each in São Paulo. Spanish emigrants keep arriving in São Paulo. On the 11th of this month, 698 Spanish emigrants arrived, who will be sent to coffee fazendas to try not only to drink it, but, most importantly, to work around it under the agreement of the fazenda 'encarregado' or 'feitor'.

Dissatisfied, as reported by São Paulo newspapers, the first emigrants from Poland are expected to arrive.

We ask our São Paulo Ukrainians to follow the newspapers, to visit those emigrants and inform us of the results of those visits, Editorial Office 'Ukr. Khl.'

On the 11th, in São Paulo, a bloody scene unfolded in the American consulate, the victim of which was the American consul Sterdo Gofort, who received 4 serious wounds from another American citizen, David Ward.

The reason for this scene was formalities when obtaining a passport.

Mystery. The Curitiba branch of 'Banco do Brasil' recently sent 600 contos to Rio to teach. In Rio, that package was opened and, oh, wonder... there was no money in the package. They returned that package to Curitiba and are now looking for the money.

The police have already questioned many people, looking, thinking, guessing, but the money trail is gone.

Look for wind in the field now!

Qualification of 'electors'. After the elections on the 24th of this month, in which the Democratic Party of Paraná unexpectedly showed considerable strength for itself, the people of the Paraná party: Republican (governing) and Democratic (opposition) began frantically preparing for the upcoming municipal elections and others. It would be interesting here that the opposition developed widely and, using the unrest and laxity, is growing in strength. Qualified people are known in this party. If anyone is in it, or wants to be an elector, let them declare it.

Considering the issue of the trade bureau in Brazil, more elements can be brought in. There is a lack of a trade bureau here that would deal with commodity exchange, as well as another that would deal with trade relations. According to Brazilian law, it would be possible to conduct commodity exchange, namely: wood, yerba, and other things. In addition, there are many illiterate people. This is one of the reasons why the Brazilian government does not promote emigration to Brazil. Despite Brazil's wealth, trade exchange is not developing. On Ukrainian affairs.

Materials on the history of the Hetmanate. Research associate of the Academy of Sciences in Paris, Ilko Borshchak, who also edits 'Ukrainian Herald' in Paris, informed the Academy that he found a hitherto unknown document of the new scribe of Hetman Mazepa and his nephew Voinarovsky in the Paris National Museum. The document concerns the times of Mazepa's march to the side of Charles of Sweden, when negotiations were underway with Sweden for Ukraine's transition to independence from Russia. In historical circles of the Academy of Sciences in Paris, work has already been prepared on the history of Russian political thought.

Ukrainian scholars protest against the oppression of Belarusians and Ukrainians in Poland. The 2nd All-Ukrainian Congress of Scientific Workers, on behalf of 4 thousand professors and lecturers and workers of scientific research institutions of Ukraine, decided to raise the voice of indignation and protest to scientific workers worldwide against the Warsaw oppressions of the Polish government of Piłsudski, against the unprecedented social and national oppression of Belarusians. These oppressions should provoke even greater indignation, as only a victorious revolution that broke the prison of nations and restored the independence of the Ukrainian people can give them the opportunity to develop freely. Government circles in Polish territories continue the practice of persecuting Ukrainians, Belarusians, and other national minorities. The Piłsudski government revived the methods of the same Muravyov-Vishatel and other tsarist executioners, whose hands tsarism once tried to strangle the Polish people.

Liquidation of Bachynsky's 'khrunivstvo'. Recently, Dr. Bachynsky made his last pilgrimage to Warsaw, but there, from the mouth of Vice-Premier Bartlya, he heard that further negotiations with him were futile, as he remained alone and represented no one. Bachynsky still does not intend to resign from further support of UNDO and is preparing a brochure with compromising materials against its leaders.

Also, Polish landowners and officials did everything to make the Ukrainian population hostile to Polish state thought. 'Therefore, the current courtship of Ukrainian politicians to the Bolsheviks is clever. The improvement of relations with nations in Poland is now complicated by the emigration of Ukrainians. '

From around the world.

Political isolation of Yugoslavia. Vienna. — 'Matin's' exposé on England's role in the formation of the Italo-Albanian agreement caused great dissatisfaction among Belgrade political circles with England. Some newspapers state that after the meeting between Chamberlain and Mussolini in Livorno, England gave Italy freedom in the Balkans, and evidently tasked it with paralyzing the influence of the USSR in the Balkans. Therefore, the opposition press in Yugoslavia continues the campaign for the restoration of relations with the USSR, emphasizing Yugoslavia's complete isolation.

Rome 10-3. Pope Pius XI long ago completed the awarding of the former President of Poland, Wojciechowski, with the Order of the Golden Spur, and former ministers Grabski, Rataj, and Skrzyński with the Orders of the Great Cross. (Well, how can Ukrainian Catholics not rejoice at this news? The Pope himself rewarded Grabski for closing over 2000 Ukrainian schools in Galicia – Ed. Note). League of Nations. The regular meeting of the League of Nations began on the 7th of this month. Germany participates as an equal member and has already raised the issue of Upper Silesia as one of the main points for resolution, where in recent elections to the local council, the Germans achieved a decisive victory over the Poles, declaring that cholera had brewed them against the Polish 'szlachta'. Until now, there was nothing similar. This szlachta understood very well the significance of this issue for its citizens. This testifies to a deep understanding of political life. Ukrainian political life and survival. According to the Bolsheviks, this whole matter is only now acquiring political significance. 'First of all, we must answer the question: Is it possible to create a strong and influential Ukraine abroad. But Polish society must not agree with this. Because it knows very well that the Ukrainian people have no chance of existence. If it agrees with this, it will force it to be hostile to the Polish nation. Therefore, it is impossible to allow Bolshevik agitators to spread Russophilia among the Ukrainian population. Until Ukraine is independent, the forty-million-strong Ukrainian nation will not unite and will not restore its old state traditions. Five Belarusian political prisoners in Lukiškės prison, BUT WITHOUT MONEY. Money should be sent by postal orders, or, in extreme cases, in registered letters, but in well-sealed envelopes, well-sealed among themselves, because ambassadors have plenty of foreign money. THE ADMINISTRATION IS NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR LOSS OF MONEY IN THE POST.

BLOODY REPRISAL. In a town in Polissia, on February 3rd of this year, Belarusians staged a demonstration, demanding the release of arrested Belarusian envoys. At the police's call, the demonstrators did not disperse, the police department gave volleys from them. On the spot, three people were killed, six were seriously wounded and a significant number of legionaries died from their wounds. After that massacre, the police arrested a number of people.

FROM THE ADMINISTRATION

The Administration of 'Ukrainian Farmer' appeals to all interested parties not to send money in ordinary letters, but registered ones. Or a double transfer. Either write nothing, or write only the names of those who send money. The Administration is not responsible for money loss in the mail. This is done for the benefit of the senders themselves and to avoid misunderstandings. Inform your acquaintances about this.
