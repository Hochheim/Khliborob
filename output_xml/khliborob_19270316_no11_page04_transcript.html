did I ever make a fool of myself? peace, which did not prove that the most wretched thing in the world is drunkenness, and that every drunkard makes a beast of himself, and not only a beast, but a sinful devil, who tramples the gift of the Holy Spirit, that is: reason, underfoot? – For that, boy, when you see a gathering of drunkards, do not tell them differently, but just say:

'Oh, curses on you, and shame on you, and may stones be with you and rattling, where chickens do not crow and bells do not clang, you shameless and godless ones! May God grant that such a thing never again be seen or heard in our Rus!' – People only shed tears, and then they started turning our Andrus into a target!... And it was so that the child's father barely pulled him out of the hands of the drunken godfathers, and said to him: 'What, boy, are you, out of your mind, or what? If such a thing happens to you, do not speak so badly, but say: 'God grant that such a thing be seen and heard in every village a hundred and two hundred times a year, and in every house two or three times!' That's what you should say, fool, and now go to your mother, so that there's no stench here!'

Our Andrus went on, scratching his clenched head, and suddenly a crowd of people came from a funeral, the father in front, a woman with a child behind him, and then godparents, relatives, neighbors, guests, and little Andrus to them: Oh, curses on you, and shame on you, and may stones be with you and rattling, where chickens do not crow and bells do not clang, you shameless and godless ones! May God grant that such a thing never again be seen or heard in our Rus!' – People only shed tears, and then they started turning our Andrus into a target!... And it was so that the child's father barely pulled him out of the hands of the drunken godfathers, and said to him: 'What, boy, are you, out of your mind, or what? If such a thing happens to you, do not speak so badly, but say: 'God grant that such a thing be seen and heard in every village a hundred and two hundred times a year, and in every house two or three times!' That's what you should say, fool, and now go to your mother, so that there's no stench here!'

Our Andrus went on, and no longer scratching, because there was nothing left for him to scratch, such a battered wretch he was, and then they carry a corpse, and Andrus from afar to them: 'God grant that such a thing be seen and heard, in every village a hundred and two hundred times a year, and in every house two or three times!' People stopped and just wanted to give our Andrus a new scolding, but the priest did not let them and said to him: 'What is it, poor soul, that you have said? – The second time, if such a thing happens to you, take off your hat, cross yourself, and say:

'With the righteous spirits, Christ God, and faithful rest in bright paradise, in eternal peace, where angels sing, where the Saints abide!' – that's what you should say, son, and not babble such things that it's shameful to listen to. Get out!'

Our Andrus went on weeping, and then two old women sat on the porch, lamenting, and Andrus immediately took off his hat, crossed himself, and with a full mouth: 'With the righteous spirits, Christ God, and faithful rest in bright paradise, in eternal peace, where angels sing, where the Saints abide!' – that's what you should say, son, and not babble such things that it's shameful to listen to. Get out!'
