Research Agent

Ask questions about the archive in Ukrainian, English, or Brazilian Portuguese. The agent retrieves the relevant passages from the digitized corpus and — when AI synthesis is enabled below — writes a grounded answer with inline citations. Without it, matching passages are quoted verbatim. Either way, every claim links to its source. Covers the pilot corpus (2 issues) only.
