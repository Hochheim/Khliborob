Research Agent

Ask questions about the archive in Ukrainian, English, or Brazilian Portuguese. This runs entirely in your browser: it matches your question against the digitized corpus and returns the original passages verbatim, with full citations — no server, no AI paraphrasing, nothing that can hallucinate a source. It only covers the pilot corpus currently on this site (2 issues).

👋 Welcome to the Khliborob Research Agent. Ask about people, places, or events mentioned in the digitized issues of Хлібороб / O Lavrador — e.g. "Mazepa", "American consulate", "Bachynsky". This searches the original transcripts directly and quotes them back to you with a source citation.

Answers are generated from the digitized corpus only. All claims cite their source.
